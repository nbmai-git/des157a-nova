Learning Journal

Images - 10/27/26

One takeaway from this article is that its interesting how the advice for intrigue in photography can sound a lot like journalism; advice such as "ask open ended questions", "be detectives", and "build on others observations" all sound geared towards an interview or observation piece. Photography is a form of documentary afterall, so the narrative framework applies here.

Visual thinking in photography is not so different than other forms of commentary. Especially when combined with interactive elements, you can really make a user experience a journey. I think the strength of photographs come from how they can be interpreted, where its really "show not tell".

Windows Design - 10/15/26

Pop-up windows are something we commonly encounter on websites, they open interesting potential for interactions or other ways to quickly capture attention. The article "Best Practices for Modals" goes into detail about the design strategies used for these window. The section about the anatomy terms of a window was new info to me, such as "escape hatch" for an exit window, and shadow for an overlay that goes behind the window for contrast.

Forms Design

Forms design is not something that's often on my mind, but it is true that it's one of the most commonly used ways of gathering information. As a common user of forms, many of this article's practices made sense to me. Things like making sure to distinguish required and non required fields, labels, order of questioning, etc all help to make the process more convenient for the user, which is essential for getting user data n the first place.

Some of the practices I had not considered from the designers perspective, such as default values, box field sizes, highlighting first-input fields as a focal point, and making a form tab-ready. As I transition from being a forms user to a forms designer, it's important that I have these considerations for how these layouts are created.

One website form that I enjoy the UX design of is the Carbon footprint calculator, for its linear and understandable field inputs and pleasing animated design.
